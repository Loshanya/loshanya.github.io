Trendydice Market Expansion & Growth Strategy

  • Tech Stack: Market Research, Excel, Strategy, Data Analysis
  • Project URL: Project Link

Developed a market expansion and growth strategy for Trendydice, focusing on identifying high-potential college markets and designing a scalable go-to-market approach across 500 colleges.

Analyzed the customer funnel to identify key conversion gaps and opportunities for growth, with a focus on improving acquisition, engagement, and overall customer conversion.

Built a growth model targeting ₹5.31 Cr in revenue, supported by a 4:1 LTV/CAC ratio and 45% blended gross margins, while identifying strategies to improve conversions from 1.5% to 4%.
