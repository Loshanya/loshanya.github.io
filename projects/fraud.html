Fraud Detection & Risk Scoring

  • Tech Stack: Python, Pandas, Scikit-learn, XGBoost

Analyzed 6.36M+ financial transactions to build a machine learning-based fraud detection and risk scoring system, addressing the challenges of highly imbalanced transaction data.

Compared multiple classification models including Logistic Regression, Decision Tree, Random Forest, Gradient Boosting, and XGBoost to identify the most effective approach for fraud detection.

Tuned the XGBoost model to achieve 95.2% fraud recall despite fraud representing only 0.129% of total transactions, improving the system's ability to identify high-risk transactions.
